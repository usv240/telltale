# Progress posts (#OpenCVComp26)

Post one every few days from the build phase onward; tag @OpenCV and use #OpenCVComp26. Each
post has one picture or clip and one number.

1. **It sees what you cannot.** Overlay of the Mukilteo bluff on 9 Dec 2015: the slide body
   moving 0.9 px in a day, four to seven times its quiet baseline, in public USGS imagery.
   Classical OpenCV 5 only, no neural network. #OpenCVComp26

2. **A hundredth of a pixel.** Our sub-pixel correlation measures 0.01 px mean error on the
   synthetic sweep across noise and lighting. That is about 1 mm at 100 m with a phone camera.
   Chart from E1. #OpenCVComp26

3. **The skeptic.** When the stabiliser flags a camera event, an agent on Amazon Bedrock
   re-runs OpenCV on the stored images, checks coherence and residuals, and closes four out of
   four lens-distortion events as false alarms without paging anyone. Trace screenshot.
   #OpenCVComp26

4. **Graviton does the heavy lifting.** One c8g.large processes 2,000 epochs an hour; about
   26,000 nights of daily monitoring per dollar. E6 table. #OpenCVComp26 #AWS

5. **The collapse.** Clip of the tabletop slope failing inside the forecast window, dashboard
   beside it. (After the rig run.) #OpenCVComp26

6. **It is live.** Dashboard link, the real-slope camera, and the instrument-health panel
   showing which OpenCV build ran on which instance. #OpenCVComp26
